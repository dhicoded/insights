Meera Sharma

Senior Mathematics Teacher

About Me

Hello! I'm Meera, and I believe mathematics is not about memorizing formulas but understanding concepts and developing logical thinking. With over 8 years of experience teaching students from Classes 1-10, I've helped thousands of students discover the joy of mathematics.

My Teaching Approach

  • Using real-world examples to explain mathematical concepts
  • Making abstract concepts tangible through visual learning
  • Creating a patient and encouraging environment for all learners
  • Providing personalized attention to address individual learning gaps

Subjects I Teach

  • Mathematics (Classes 1-10)
  • Arithmetic Fundamentals
  • Algebra and Geometry
  • Problem-Solving Techniques

My Qualifications

  • M.Sc. in Mathematics from Delhi University
  • B.Ed. from National Council of Educational Research and Training
  • Certified by CBSE for mathematics instruction
  • Regular workshops on innovative teaching methods

Fun Fact

I love using cooking and shopping scenarios to teach fractions! Math is everywhere once you know where to look.
