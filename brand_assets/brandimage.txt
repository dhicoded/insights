3. Logo Usage
Section title: Logo Usage

Primary logo: use the full “Insights by Minju Jacob” logo on white or very light backgrounds.

Clear space: keep at least the height of the orange dot around all sides of the logo.

Minimum size: do not use the logo smaller than 25 mm width in print or 120 px width on screen.

Do:

Use the original colors.

Place on clean, uncluttered backgrounds.

Don’t:

Don’t stretch, rotate, or crop the logo.

Don’t change colors or add shadows/outlines.

Don’t place on busy or similar-colored backgrounds.

5. Typography
Heading font (Brand personality: elegant + modern)

Recommended: a script or modern display font similar to the “Insights” word in the logo for large titles only.

Usage: Page titles, big headers, posters.

Body font (Clean and easy to read)

Recommended:

Sans-serif options: Poppins, Montserrat, Lato, or Open Sans.

Usage: Body text, worksheets, presentations, website content.

Code font (for coding content)

Recommended:

Fira Code, JetBrains Mono, or Consolas.

Usage: Code snippets, terminal output, programming tasks.

Basic rules:

Headings: use Deep Navy.

Subheadings: Teal or Navy.

Body text: Dark Grey or Navy on white.

6. Imagery & Icons
Style:

Use clean, minimal illustrations of:

Laptops, robots, puzzles, graphs, geometric shapes.

Prefer vector icons with rounded corners and line-based style.

Avoid overly realistic or cluttered photos.

Example imagery guidelines:

Show students collaborating, problem solving, and presenting.

Use backgrounds that match brand colors (navy, teal, white).

7. Tone of Voice
How the brand should “sound” in all text:

Clear and simple (avoid jargon, explain terms).

Encouraging and positive (focus on growth and effort).

Structured and logical (step-by-step explanations).

Slightly playful, especially for younger students.

Example statements to include:

“Every big idea starts with one clear insight.”

“Mistakes are steps toward mastery.”

“We code, we calculate, we understand.”
