Anita Gupta

Mathematics Educator

About Me

Hello! I'm Anita, and I have a deep love for making mathematics accessible and enjoyable for every student. I understand that math can be challenging for many children, and my mission is to change that perception. I use creative methods and real-life applications to show students that math is both useful and fun!

My Teaching Approach

  • Step-by-step concept building from basics
  • Using manipulatives and visual aids
  • Relating math to everyday life situations
  • Creating a stress-free learning environment

Subjects I Teach

  • Mathematics (Classes 1-8)
  • Times Tables Mastery
  • Mental Math Techniques
  • Exam Preparation (CBSE/ICSE)

My Qualifications

  • B.Sc. in Mathematics from Banaras Hindu University
  • B.Ed. with specialization in primary education
  • 10+ years of teaching experience
  • Trained in Cambridge math pedagogy

Fun Fact

I can calculate the day of the week for any date in history! Math magic tricks are my specialty in class.
