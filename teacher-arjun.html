About Me

Hi! I'm Arjun, and I specialize in teaching coding and logical thinking to young learners. I believe that everyone can learn to code - it's just like learning any other skill, starting with the basics and building up progressively. My classes are interactive, hands-on, and focused on building real projects.

My Teaching Approach

  • Hands-on coding from day one
  • Logical thinking and problem decomposition
  • Collaborative learning through pair programming
  • Building a portfolio of projects

Subjects I Teach

  • Python Programming
  • Scratch & Block-based Coding
  • Data Structures Basics
  • Competitive Programming Introduction

My Qualifications

  • M.Tech in Computer Science from IISc Bangalore
  • 6+ years in tech industry and teaching
  • Google Certified Educator
  • Regular contributor to coding competitions
